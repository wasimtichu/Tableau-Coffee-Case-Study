# Tableau-Coffee-Case-Study

https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/md.wasim.akhtar3999/viz/CoffeeCaseStudy_17246708160580/AreaCodesOfCalifornia

1. Compared Budgeted Sales and Budgeted Profit with Actual Sales and Profit.
2. Created a Top N parameter to identify the top-performing states based on Profit for Major Market sizes.
3. Calculated the percentage contribution of Central Market Sales in both Major and Small Markets.
4. Analyzed the correlation between Marketing Costs and Sales across various products.
5. Identified area codes in California with sales exceeding $6,000 and visually distinguished them from others.
6. Displayed market-wise sales on a geographical map and highlighted the market with the highest sales.
7. Analyzed the Top 2 and Bottom 2 states in terms of Profit and represented them using a geographical map.
