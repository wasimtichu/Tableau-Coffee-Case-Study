# Sales & Market Performance Analysis Of A Coffee Chain <br>

I conducted a comprehensive sales and profitability analysis to gain insights into revenue trends, market performance, and key business drivers.

Key Analyses Conducted:

1. Compared Budgeted vs. Actual Sales and Profit to assess financial performance and identify key variances.
2. Developed a dynamic Top N parameter to highlight the top-performing states based on Profit within Major Market sizes.
3. Calculated the percentage contribution of Central Market Sales in both Major and Small Markets, providing a clearer view of regional impact.
4. Analyzed the correlation between Marketing Costs and Sales across various products to evaluate the effectiveness of marketing investments.
4. Identified California area codes with sales exceeding $6,000 and visually distinguished them for easy analysis.
5. Mapped market-wise sales on a geographical dashboard, highlighting the market with the highest sales for better regional insights.
6. Ranked the Top 2 and Bottom 2 states based on Profit and represented them visually on a geographical map for clear comparison.

This data-driven approach provided valuable insights into sales trends, profitability, and market performance, helping optimize strategic decisions.
